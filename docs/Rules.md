# UI/UX & Development Rules

## Design Constraints
- **NO Cheap Children's Look:** Avoid excessive rainbow colors, childish clip art, generic stock photos, excessive gradients, clutter, and overly cartoonish UI.
- **Aesthetic:** Premium modern startup combined with an interactive game world.
- **Visuals:** Use original CSS/SVG/vector-style illustrations or generated visual assets.

## Functionality Constraints
- **No Shared Routes for Missions:** Every mission must have an explicitly unique route (e.g., `/world/marketplace`).
- **No Generic Mission Components:** Each mission must be a completely unique interactive experience, not just a reskinned generic template.
- **Interactive, not Passive:** Optimize for Understanding, Application, and Creation over Content Consumption and Scores.
- **India-First Context:** Use ₹ currency, Indian names (e.g., Aarav, Maya), and contexts naturally. Include a visible language switcher (English, Hindi, Marathi).

## Tone and Messaging
- Focus on "Learning isn't a lesson. It's an adventure."
- Emphasize the core loop: LEARN -> PLAY -> DO -> CREATE.
- Do NOT make dishonest claims about competitors (e.g., "We are better than Khan Academy").
- Do NOT claim the app scientifically diagnoses personality or intelligence. Focus on "emerging strengths".
- "Rewards encourage learning. They don't replace it." (Avoid casino-like addiction mechanics).

## Quality Assurance
- Must work on Desktop (priority), Laptop, Tablet, Mobile.
- Animations must be smooth.
- No heavy assets (lazy load if necessary).
- Zero console errors in the final build.
